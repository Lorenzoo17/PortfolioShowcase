Project 06 · Master’s thesis · 2026

Real-World Guided Depth Super-Resolution

My master’s thesis studies guided depth super-resolution using real sensor measurements. I developed BAF-Net, a boundary-aware fusion network that combines metric depth reconstruction with RGB and Depth Anything V2 guidance to recover fine detail and sharper object boundaries. It reaches 4.011 cm RMSE on the official TOFDSR test set.

Computer vision Depth super-resolution BAF-Net Depth Anything V2 Real-world ToF data
